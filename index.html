Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

In short

Pokus keeps everything in your own browser. No accounts, no tracking, and nothing is sent to any server.

What we store

Your block list, whether a focus session is on and when it started, and whether you've seen the welcome screen. All of it is saved in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device. Removing Pokus from your browser deletes all of it.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your browsing history, personal details, or usage analytics. We don't sell, rent, or share data with anyone, because we don't have any.

Browser permissions

Pokus checks the address of the page you're opening, and of tabs that are already open when a focus session starts, only to compare it against your block list and redirect you back to focus when it matches. That check happens locally, in real time, and nothing is recorded.

Feedback

If you send feedback through our form, we receive only what you choose to write there, your email if you add one, and the version of Pokus you're using. The form is run by Tally, and we use what you send only to reply to you and improve Pokus.

Children's privacy

Pokus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone in that age group.

Links to other sites

The extension may link to external pages, such as the feedback form, the Chrome Web Store, or Buy Me a Coffee. These only open when you click them, and those sites have their own privacy policies, which we don't control.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Continued use of Pokus means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at stay.pokus@gmail.com.
